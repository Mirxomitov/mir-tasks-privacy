Mir Tasks – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · App: Mir Tasks (uz.mir.tasks) · Developer: Tohir Mirxomitov

Summary

Mir Tasks is a to-do list app that works completely offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The tasks you create (title, note, due date, priority and completion status) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the app. The app does not request the Internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

If you have Android's system backup enabled, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual tasks inside the app, clear completed tasks, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
